## How Icons Are Used

1. **Robot Icon**: Displayed at the start position, moves along the pathfinding solution
2. **Obstacle Icon**: Optional visual representation of barriers (instead of colored blocks)
3. **Goal Icon**: Displayed at the destination/goal position

All icons are loaded dynamically from the `themes/` directory .
